Why fiber laser

Engraved, not printed

Printing and stickers wear off. A fiber laser marks the metal itself, so your design lasts as long as the product does.

Permanent

Won't peel, fade or scratch off with normal use.

Precise

Fine text and detailed artwork down to fractions of a millimeter.
